## A visual grammar, not a filter

Simple forms, diagonals and the contrast of red, black and paper gave the Russian avant-garde a visual language tied to the upheavals of its time. In Russia 1917, that reference matters only when it helps organise information.

Railways must guide the eye across the map. Political menus need a clear hierarchy between a decision, its costs and its consequences. Aesthetics then become part of the gameplay.
