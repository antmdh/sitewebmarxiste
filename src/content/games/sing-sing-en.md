## Seeing colonisation from the ground

Sing Sing Valley moves the French war in Indochina into a science-fiction setting. This distance lets the player enter through character and story before gradually recognising the political and economic structures of colonial occupation.

The project is currently in pre-production. Its mechanics, art direction and narrative scope are provisional.
