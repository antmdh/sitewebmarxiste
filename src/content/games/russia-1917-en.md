## A revolution on several fronts

Russia 1917 is a mobile strategy game about the Russian Civil War. Beginning in November 1917, you lead the Bolshevik camp through foreign intervention, national conflicts and economic collapse.

### Military choices

Cities and railways shape the map. Moving units, defending Moscow and Petrograd, and responding to historically timed factions turn logistics into the core of play.

### Political and economic choices

Forty-three historical debates are planned, from the Decree on Peace to the creation of the USSR. Every decision changes what is possible on the map, in the economy and in the final account of the country you have built.
